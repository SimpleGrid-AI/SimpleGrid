tile-reveal.js

Hover tile image reveal

An image split into a grid of tiles that assemble from the bottom row upward. Every element carrying data-tile-reveal is picked up automatically; the same paused timeline is driven either by hover or by ScrollTrigger.

Hover, 6 columns, from the bottom

Point at the image to assemble it, move away to take it apart. data-cols="6" data-from="end" data-axis="y" data-trigger="hover"

scroll on ↓

Scroll, 8 columns, from the centre

Driven by ScrollTrigger with toggleActions:"play none none reverse", so it assembles on the way down and comes apart on the way back up. data-cols="8" data-from="center" data-trigger="scroll"
