Our Story

The books were never the problem. The floor was invisible.

We ran a multi-stage production facility with hundreds of people on the floor. The business grew to $30M — on one person’s memory, group chats and a wall of spreadsheets.

Twice we bought an ERP to fix it. Twice we paid six figures, spent months in workshops, and watched the floor quietly go back to Excel — because the ERP was built to close the books, and our problem was never the books.

Accounting is standardized by law. Operations are not. So we built the operations cloud we kept trying to buy.
